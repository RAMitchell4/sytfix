Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

SytFix ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use sytfix.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you request an audit or contact us, we collect the information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number, business website, and any details you share about your project.

We also collect standard analytics data automatically — pages visited, time on site, and general location — through tools like Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use our site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the website audit and Lindy Score™ report you requested
  • To respond to your inquiries and follow up on audit results
  • To improve our website and service offerings
  • To send occasional updates about our services, which you can opt out of at any time

How We Protect Your Information

We use industry-standard security practices to protect the data you share with us. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party tools such as Google Analytics for site performance tracking. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing alex@sytfix.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to alex@sytfix.com.
