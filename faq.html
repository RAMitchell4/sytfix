Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers. No sales-speak.

The questions every business owner actually asks before signing off on a website fix — answered plainly, including the ones with less flattering answers.

How much does SytFix cost?

Pricing depends on what your audit actually finds. A site with one weak dimension costs less to fix than one with five broken ones. You get a fixed quote after your free audit, before any work starts — no surprise invoice at the end.

How long does a typical fix take?

Most remediations ship within 7 to 14 days from the day you approve the quote. Larger rebuilds involving significant conversion or structural work can run closer to three weeks. Your quote includes a specific timeline, not a vague estimate.

Will my website go down during the fix?

No. We work in a staging copy of your site, not the live version. Your current site keeps running and taking calls normally until the fixed version is tested and ready — then it's usually a single same-day switch.

Do I need to switch hosting or platforms?

Almost never. We work within your existing platform — WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, or custom code — unless your audit reveals the platform itself is the core problem. If that's the case, we'll explain exactly why before recommending a change.

What exactly is the Lindy Score™?

A 0–100 score calculated across seven weighted dimensions: speed, mobile experience, SEO health, security, conversion architecture, code health, and accessibility. You get your starting score with the free audit and a target score with your fix quote, so you see the actual gap being closed.

Who owns the code after the fix?

You do, fully, once the engagement is paid in full. There's no ongoing licensing fee to keep using what we built, and no lock-in requiring you to keep paying us for access to your own site.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance after the fix?

Yes, as an optional add-on, not a requirement. Some clients want us monitoring uptime, security patches, and score drift monthly. Others prefer a one-time fix and full independence afterward. Both are completely fine with us.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Every quote includes a revision window after delivery specifically for this. If something doesn't match what was scoped, we fix it at no extra charge within that window. We'd rather get it right than argue about it.

Will this actually improve my Google ranking?

We can't promise a specific ranking position — no honest provider can, since Google's algorithm and your competitors are both moving targets. What we can promise is that the technical and structural barriers actually within our control — speed, schema, crawlability — get fixed properly.
